About

Most founders I notice aren't struggling because their product is bad or their market is wrong. They're struggling because the way they're running the company hasn't kept up with where the company is.

Every decision goes through them. The team waits. Work piles up. Growth starts feeling like chaos instead of progress.

That's not a people problem. That's a systems problem.

I get inside the business, find where the friction is actually coming from, and fix it. Not a deck. Not a recommendation. The actual fix. I work based on outcomes, not time. I move as fast as the work allows without compromising on quality, and I'm not leaving until what we agreed to get done is done.

No retainers. No open ended roles. Every engagement is scoped to a specific outcome with a price tied to that scope. You know exactly what you're getting and what it costs before anything starts.

How an engagement works
  • 01
    First conversation We meet: over coffee, in your office, or on a call. You tell me what's happening. Some founders come in with a clear outcome. Some don't know what's wrong. Some think they know but the real problem is elsewhere. All three are fine. I listen for what's not being said as much as what is.
  • 02
    I get inside the company After our conversation I take one day to one week inside the business: observing how decisions get made, where work stalls, what the team dynamic reveals. This is where the real constraint shows up. Not what you described. What's actually happening.
  • 03
    Second meeting. The diagnosis I come back with what I found. We discuss the actual problems, align on the outcome that matters most, and agree on what success looks like before anything else happens.
  • 04
    Scope Report I deliver a written report defining the required changes, the rationale, the price tied to that scope, and the completion condition. You approve before work begins. No surprises.
  • 05
    I execute Hands-on work with weekly progress reports. I move as fast as the work allows without compromising quality. I don't leave until what we agreed to get done is done.
  • 06
    Clean exit Once the outcome is achieved I deliver a Closing Report and exit. No retainer. No dependency. If a new constraint surfaces later, we start fresh.
Who I work with

I work with founders, CEOs, and co-founders: and anyone else inside the company who has enough influence to drive real change or get the right people in the room. Title matters less than authority.

The right fit is a company that has clarity on what it sells and has moved past the earliest stage. Product is defined, there's some traction, and the focus has shifted from building to scaling. That's where operational friction tends to show up most.

If the business is still finding its footing on what to build or who to sell to, the timing probably isn't right. But once that's clear and execution starts feeling harder than it should: that's the conversation worth having.
